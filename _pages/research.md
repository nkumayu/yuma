<p class="eyebrow">Research interests</p>
<p class="page-lead">From observable leakage to protected implementations.</p>

My research concerns the implementation security of post-quantum digital signature algorithms. I study side-channel attacks, leakage analysis, and masking countermeasures under the supervision of **Prof. Meiqin Wang (王美琴)** at **Shandong University**.

<div class="research-areas">
  <section class="research-area" aria-labelledby="attacks-heading">
    <span class="index" aria-hidden="true">01 / ATTACKS</span>
    <div><h2 id="attacks-heading">Side-channel attacks</h2><p>My interests include how physical observations of a signature implementation can reveal information about secret-dependent computations. I am interested in connecting an attacker's observations with the operations performed by the implementation.</p><p class="research-question">Research question: Where can a post-quantum signature implementation expose sensitive information?</p></div>
  </section>
  <section class="research-area" aria-labelledby="leakage-heading">
    <span class="index" aria-hidden="true">02 / ANALYSIS</span>
    <div><h2 id="leakage-heading">Leakage analysis</h2><p>I am interested in characterizing leakage and understanding how implementation choices influence side-channel behavior. This includes examining sensitive intermediate values and the evidence needed to assess implementation leakage.</p><p class="research-question">Research question: How can leakage be identified, characterized, and interpreted?</p></div>
  </section>
  <section class="research-area" aria-labelledby="masking-heading">
    <span class="index" aria-hidden="true">03 / DEFENSE</span>
    <div><h2 id="masking-heading">Masking countermeasures</h2><p>I explore masking as a means of protecting sensitive intermediate computations in post-quantum signatures. My interests include the relationship between a masking design and its implementation-level behavior.</p><p class="research-question">Research question: How can sensitive computations be protected in practice?</p></div>
  </section>
</div>
